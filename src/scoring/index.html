Scoring

How SEO Agent scores your website

Every audit ends with a website health score, and the geo command adds a GEO score for AI search. Both are out of 100, both are built from published research, and both show you what to fix first.

Why AI citations matter now

Fewer people click through to websites when an AI answer appears. Pew Research Center found Google users were less likely to click a link when an AI summary was shown, and Ahrefs found AI Overviews reduced clicks by 58%.

When AI tools answer your customers' questions, the businesses they quote get the visibility. The GEO score shows how ready each page is to be one of them.

The website health score

The health score combines the main areas of a full SEO audit. Each category is scored, weighted and combined into one number, with the top priorities listed underneath.

Category Weight
Content quality 23%
Technical SEO 22%
On-page SEO 20%
Schema and structured data 10%
Performance (Core Web Vitals) 10%
AI search readiness 10%
Images 5%

The GEO score

The GEO score rates each page out of 100 across four groups. It combines an AI review of the page (60%) with rule-based checks of its server HTML (40%), the version AI crawlers actually read.

Group What we check Points
Answerability The page answers the questions it should win, near the top, with headings written as questions and the obvious follow-ups covered 40
Extractability Passages make sense on their own, at a length AI tools can quote, with clear headings, lists and tables 36
Evidence Statistics, named sources, first-hand detail and structured data 20
Freshness Visible dates and named authors 4

Alongside the page scores, the GEO report checks the site as a whole: whether AI crawlers are allowed in, whether pages work without JavaScript, how often the brand is mentioned elsewhere, and whether an llms.txt file exists. Google has said llms.txt is not needed, so its absence is reported but not scored.

The research behind the GEO score

Answer the question early

Zyppy's AI Citation Ranking Factors analysis rated query-answer match 9.2 out of 10 and having the answer near the top of the page 8.8 out of 10, two of its strongest factors.

Back up your claims

The GEO paper from Princeton and partners, published at KDD 2024, found adding statistics improved visibility in AI answers by up to around 40% in its experiments. Citing sources and adding quotations also helped.

Keep it current

Ahrefs analysed around 17 million URLs cited by AI assistants and found they were 25.7% fresher on average than pages in standard Google results.

The four groups follow a GEO scoring framework built on these three studies. We update the scoring as new research is published.

What the scores are not

The scores show how well a site and its pages follow the patterns that research links to rankings and AI citations. They are estimates to help you decide what to fix first, not a measure of current AI visibility or a promise of results.

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO?+
GEO stands for generative engine optimisation. It is the practice of making content easy for AI tools such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search and Perplexity to find, understand and quote.
Is the GEO score the same as the health score?+
No. The health score covers the whole of SEO, with AI search readiness as one category. The GEO score looks only at how likely each page is to be quoted in AI answers. Strong SEO still helps, because many AI tools draw on search results.
Which commands produce the scores?+
Every /seo audit gives a health score. /seo geo gives the GEO score, and /seo geo --site scores every key page and ranks them by what to fix first.
Can I put the scores in client reports?+
Yes. SEO Agent Pro adds both scores to white-label PDF reports with your logo, colours and tone of voice.
Does a high GEO score guarantee AI citations?+
No. Nobody can guarantee that an AI tool will quote a page, and results vary by platform and industry. The score shows how well a page follows the patterns that published research links to citations, so you know what to fix first.
